Seen through the work of the United Nations

First, what is the United Nations?

The United Nations (UN) is an organization of countries, founded in 1945 right after World War II. It has 193 member states, and its headquarters is in New York City. It was created so that nations would talk and work together instead of fighting, which fits our school theme well. The three parts of the theme are below, each connected to what the UN does.

Restoring Trust, Embracing Transformation: MCSians Building a World That Serves All.

Part 1

Restoring Trust

After World War II, countries no longer trusted each other. The UN was founded in 1945 to rebuild that trust, and it still does so in a few ways:

  • The UN Charter: the founding agreement, in which countries promised to settle disputes peacefully.
  • The Security Council: responsible for international peace and security.
  • Peacekeepers: UN personnel who help keep the peace in areas of conflict.
  • The International Court of Justice: settles legal disputes between countries with the law, not weapons.

Trust grows when people keep their promises. The same is true in school.

Part 2

Embracing Transformation

The world keeps changing, and so do its problems. In 2015, all UN member states adopted the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), a plan to change the world by 2030. Some examples:

  • Goal 1: No Poverty
  • Goal 2: Zero Hunger
  • Goal 4: Quality Education
  • Goal 13: Climate Action
  • Goal 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Embracing transformation means not being afraid of change, and working to make it change for the better.

Part 3

A World That Serves All

A world that serves all means no one is left out. The UN works on this through:

  • Human rights: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, adopted in 1948, says everyone is born free and equal in dignity and rights.
  • Humanitarian aid: food, shelter and medicine in emergencies.
  • Leaving no one behind: the promise of the Sustainable Development Goals.

The Philippines is also part of this story: it was one of the original UN members in 1945.

What can MCSians do?

You don't have to be a country to help build this kind of world:

  1. Restore trust by being honest and keeping your word.
  2. Embrace transformation by learning new things, even when they are hard.
  3. Serve all by treating everyone fairly and helping the people around you.
